Treasury Board Mandatory Training Inventory

This inventory of Mandatory Training (the inventory) contains courses that have been determined to be mandatory by the Secretary of the Treasury Board under the authority of section 2.3.1 of the Policy on People Management. Deputy Heads have the authority under section 12(1) (a) of the Financial Administration Act to determine additional learning, training and development requirements of persons employed within their organizations.

Employees should direct questions about mandatory training to their human resources (HR) sector. Senior-level corporate HR personnel may direct queries to the appropriate inbox listed below.

Table 1 Note 1

For the purposes of the Directive on Mandatory Training, “employees of the core public administration” includes:

everyone employed in the core public administration including indeterminate and term employees, students, and casual workers.
anyone else performing work on behalf of the employer (for example contract work, Interchange Canada assignments), as determined by the deputy head of the organization.
