Welcome to the HR Policy Advisory System

This system synthesizes guidance from DND civilian HR policy instruments. Submit your question below to receive well-referenced policy advice from our specialist agents.

Decision support system only — not for making operational decisions. Authority and accountability remains with human users at all times.

Example questions:

  • What are the requirements for staffing a bilingual position at DND?
  • What is the process for handling a workplace grievance?
  • What accommodations are required for an employee with a disability?

Your question will be routed to relevant specialist agents for analysis. Press Shift+Enter for a new line.
